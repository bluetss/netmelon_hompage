Language is more than something to translate. It is a way of thinking. People learn a new language not just to swap words from one language to another, but to form thoughts, express themselves, and see more choices in front of them. Netmelon builds speaking-training technology that helps people move past language barriers so they can learn, work, and build relationships with more freedom.

Mission

Why we exist

Netmelon turns language learning into practical speaking training, so people can stop leaving a language on the page and start thinking, speaking, and connecting with the world in it.

Vision

The world we want to build

We want to build a world where language never decides who gets to learn, work, belong, or take the next opportunity.

Core Values

How we work

#1

Make People Speak

We measure our work by whether users actually open their mouths and speak.

#2

Think Beyond Translation

We see language not as word-for-word conversion, but as a wider way to think and express yourself.

#3

Freedom Through Language

We help people gain more choices, opportunities, and confidence by thinking and speaking in a new language.

#4

Technology for Training

We build technology that supports repetition, practice, and real speaking, not just more explanations.

#5

Build from Truth

We build on verified content, accurate data, and consistent messages.

#6

From Speaking to Action

We want speaking practice to lead to real learning, work, relationships, and opportunity.
