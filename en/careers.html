Netmelon Careers Detail Page

This page is for candidates considering joining Netmelon. You can find candidate profile, application method, and hiring inquiry process.

Who We Are Looking For

  • People interested in planning and operating AI education software.
  • People motivated to solve classroom workflow problems through products.
  • People with domain experience in English learning, quizzes, or learning automation.

How We Work

  • Practical collaboration from problem definition to operations.
  • Fast improvement cycles based on user observation.
  • Clear role separation between company and product channels.

How to Apply

  • Email: netmelon@netmelonai.com
  • Subject line: [Careers Inquiry] Role Name
  • Include resume/CV and portfolio link

Reference Links

  • Company site: netmelonai.com/en
  • Naepopquiz app: naepopquiz.com/en
  • Naepopquiz Studio: studio.naepopquiz.com

Applicant Privacy

  • Application materials and contact details are used only for hiring review, interview scheduling, and result notices.
  • We do not request information unrelated to hiring, such as family details, origin, property status, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
  • After the hiring process ends, applicant data is deleted within a reasonable period unless retention is legally or operationally required.
  • Applicants may request correction, deletion, or document return by emailing netmelon@netmelonai.com.

Hiring Process

  • Typical process: resume review, role interview, assignment or portfolio review, and final discussion.
  • Assignments are scoped to role evaluation. Separate consent is required before any other use.
  • Role scope, schedule, and compensation may change based on mutual discussion and company needs.
