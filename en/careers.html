Founding Growth & Creator Partnerships

Growth & PartnershipsShort-term paid projectTo be agreedHybrid

Build the first repeatable path from learner acquisition to speaking, payment, and creator supply.

What you will do

  • Define the first anchor learner cohort and validate the funnel from outreach and acquisition through first speech, repeat use, and payment.
  • Design and run channel and message experiments, connecting CAC, activation, paid conversion, and retention in every decision.
  • Source education, language, and content creators and own personalized outreach, meetings, commercial terms, contracts, and pilot operations.
  • Build and operate the pipeline from creator outreach to repeatable supply and learner selection or purchase.
  • Turn customer rejection, churn, and language into decision-ready evidence for weekly reviews with the founder.
  • Maintain source, usage-right, compensation, and contract records and escalate rights or trust risks immediately.

What we are looking for

  • Evidence that you personally delivered acquisition, conversion, or partnership outcomes for an app, subscription service, advertising program, or creator campaign.
  • Ability to contact unfamiliar customers or creators directly and change the target, message, or offer based on rejection evidence.
  • Ability to manage acquisition, first value, payment, and repeat use as one funnel rather than optimizing impressions or installs.
  • Ability to define the next experiment and stopping criteria with a small budget and incomplete data.
  • Ability to negotiate partner terms while protecting long-term trust.

Preferred experience

  • Hands-on performance or growth marketing experience for an app or subscription product.
  • Acquisition, CRM, or lifecycle experience in edtech or consumer subscriptions.
  • Creator sourcing, outreach, negotiation, and campaign ownership in influencer marketing or an MCN.
  • Experience measuring paid or creator channels through payment and retention.
  • Experience building in an early-stage team and stopping failed hypotheses with evidence.

Founding Conversation Learning Scientist

Learning Science & ProductPaid project followed by hiring reviewTo be agreedHybrid

Design training and assessment that helps Korean speakers construct and sustain English conversations for real purposes and audiences.

What you will do

  • Design a conversation capability model connecting convention, function, purpose, empathy, and the difficulty of generating speech.
  • Build a progression from shadowing to recall, variation, personal explanation, persuasion, and unexpected-question response.
  • Observe real learner speech and classify failures in retrieval, discourse, fluency, interaction, and anxiety.
  • Create rubrics, assessment tasks, and before-and-after measures of speaking transfer.
  • Review AI-generated conversation tasks and feedback for linguistic validity, learning impact, and misjudgment risk.
  • Turn learner research into product requirements and prioritize them with the founder, product, and engineering teams.

What we are looking for

  • Experience applying applied linguistics, second-language acquisition, English education, or learning science in teaching, research, or a product.
  • Ability to observe Korean speakers' errors and diagnose their causes systematically.
  • Experience designing and improving task-based training, curricula, or speaking-assessment rubrics.
  • Ability to turn learner interviews and speech evidence into educational or product decisions.
  • Ability to express hypotheses as measurable experiments independently of your own English fluency.
  • Ability to validate AI output independently for linguistic validity and learning impact.

Preferred experience

  • Experience teaching, researching, or operating curricula for Korean adult English learners.
  • Experience developing edtech, speaking-training, or consumer learning products.
  • Experience with CEFR, ACTFL, or another speaking-proficiency framework and performance assessment.
  • Experience combining qualitative research, learning experiments, and basic statistical analysis.
  • Experience evaluating AI tutors, LLM feedback, or speech assessment quality.
