Naepopquiz creator guide

Learn how to add existing English teaching materials in Studio and share learning links with your subscribers. This guide covers learning support languages, shadowing, quizzes, challenge rooms, channel management and promotional links.

Open Studio

Content and learning support

Existing materials
Use scripts, teaching documents and expression lists as learning content. Text, supported document attachments and official Excel import are available.
Learning support languages
Meaning and explanations in learning support languages, including Korean and Japanese, help subscribers who find English-only explanations difficult.
Shadowing and quizzes
Connect your content to listening and speaking practice through shadowing, and quizzes that let learners check their understanding.

Challenges and assignments

Connect content to a challenge room, set shadowing missions and quiz assignments, organize goals and study schedules, and review participants’ learning activity.

Missions and assignments
Set shadowing missions, quiz assignments, due dates and result release policies.
Participation and management
Manage admission and members. Use announcements, questions, learning tips and study schedules.
Learning activity
Review shadowing and quiz activity and progress from the room owner’s view.

Promotional links and announcements

Update your channel’s featured announcement once and your older public learning content reads the same latest announcement. Promote a new course, book, resource, information session or meetup without editing each item.

Link channel resources and the announcement button to your HTTPS destination. Set the announcement’s display period or remove it whenever needed.

For example, set an announcement title, details and registration link for a new course information session. Conversation materials published last year and expression practice published last month read the same latest announcement.

Channel management and ownership

Brand and content
Manage the NPQ channel’s name, handle, photo, introduction, external links and learning content.
Stay connected
Channel subscriptions, subscribed-content feeds and new-content notifications help learners find the next lesson. Notifications respect user preferences.
Transfer ownership
Transfer a developed NPQ channel to another operator for succession or handover following a privately arranged sale.

Transfers apply to the NPQ channel and follow recipient consent, reauthentication and a seven-day waiting period. This is not a marketplace offering buyer matching, sale payments or escrow.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to move my YouTube channel?

No. Keep your existing YouTube channel and social accounts and share NPQ learning links. NPQ channel management organizes your learning materials and announcements.

Can I just paste a video URL?

The inputs described here are existing scripts, teaching materials, text, supported documents and official Excel templates. This page does not promise automatic ingestion or conversion of an entire video from its URL.

Are all features available in every language?

Availability depends on the learning support language, prepared materials and content type. Check the actual language, learning options and publication scope in the product.

Are views or sales guaranteed?

NPQ provides learning experiences and external promotional links. It does not guarantee more video views, sales or event registrations, or treat a click as a purchase or completed learning.
