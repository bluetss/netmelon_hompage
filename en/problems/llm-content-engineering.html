OpenLLM Content Engineering

How can we reliably produce high-quality language-learning content while reducing LLM calls and regeneration?

An LLM engineering problem that combines generation quality and efficiency for language learning.

Why this matters

  • Learning objectives, natural conversation, and verifiable outputs must be delivered through an efficient generation pipeline.

What we know

  • RAG, structured generation, model routing, and small-model optimization can be combined.

Questions to answer

  • How should models and retrieval strategies be assigned by task, and which evaluation sets can verify language-learning quality?

Principles and constraints

  • We do not substitute general benchmark scores for content quality.

Experience we are looking for

  • Experience in language-model training, fine-tuning, evaluation, RAG, and inference optimization
