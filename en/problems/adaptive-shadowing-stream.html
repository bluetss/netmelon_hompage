OpenPersonalization & Recommendation

How can we select the next shadowing exercise without interruption based on each learner's level and response?

A personalized practice queue that uses learning history to choose the sentence needed now.

Why this matters

  • The next selection should account for responses, mastery, review timing, and fatigue so practice continues where it is most useful.

What we know

  • Learning events, mastery estimation, spaced repetition, and recommendation techniques can be applied.

Questions to answer

  • Which learning behaviors should become signals, and how should review, new expressions, and interests be balanced?

Principles and constraints

  • We do not optimize recommendations for time spent alone.

Experience we are looking for

  • Experience in recommender systems, learning-data modeling, and audio streaming design
