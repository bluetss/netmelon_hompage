OpenLanguage Learning & Content

How should learning experiences and content be designed so people can immediately say the right English expression in any situation?

A language-learning and conversation-content problem focused on retrieving useful expressions in context.

Why this matters

  • Learning sequences and conversation scenarios must help people retrieve practiced expressions immediately in unfamiliar situations.

What we know

  • We have foundations for shadowing, recall practice, and situational variation training.

Questions to answer

  • When and at what difficulty should a learner move from imitation to immediate and adaptive speech?

Principles and constraints

  • This is not a project to prove the effectiveness of shadowing by itself.

Experience we are looking for

  • Experience in English education, applied linguistics, learning science, and conversation-content design
