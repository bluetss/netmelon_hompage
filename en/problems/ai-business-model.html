OpenAI Platform Business

How can we create and deliver AI content more efficiently while making paid access, generation credits, and content purchases worth choosing for both learners and creators?

A business-model problem connecting AI content production, paid access, generation credits, and content purchases.

Why this matters

  • We need a product structure that balances the resources used for AI content, learner value, and sustainable creator participation.

What we know

  • Model routing, caching, and staged generation can control the resources used to create content.

Questions to answer

  • How should we set AI quality, allowances, pricing, and participant rewards for each product?

Principles and constraints

  • We will not trade away learning quality for operating efficiency.

Experience we are looking for

  • Experience designing AI product packaging, pricing, unit economics, and platform incentives
