Company Website Privacy Policy

Netmelon Co., Ltd. provides this notice for personal information processed through IR requests, Open Problems proposals, and recruitment inquiries on the company website.

1. Purposes and information collected

  • IR requests: name, email, organization, title, phone number, investor type, requested materials, NDA preference, and request details — to review and respond
  • Solution proposals: name, email, proposed solution, and optional resource URL — to review and respond
  • Recruitment: contact details and application materials submitted by the applicant — to review the application and communicate the process

2. Retention

Information is retained for one year from submission and then deleted. Where retention is required by law or reasonably necessary to resolve a dispute, it may be kept separately for that period.

3. Right to refuse

You may refuse consent. If you do not consent to required items, the relevant request or proposal cannot be submitted.

4. Deletion and safeguards

Information is deleted using methods that prevent recovery after the retention period or purpose ends. Appropriate safeguards include access controls, protection in transit, and separation of public intake paths.

5. Your rights and contact

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing. Contact: netmelon@netmelonai.com

6. Changes

Material changes will be announced on the company website before they take effect.

Effective date: the approved production publication date
